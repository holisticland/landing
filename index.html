A UK marketplace for holistic wellbeing

Sell here without rebuilding your shop.

Holistic Land brings independent UK sellers of holistic and wellbeing products together in one storefront. If you already trade on Shopify, Etsy, eBay or Amazon, you connect that shop rather than start again — your listings come across, and your stock stays in step so the same item is never sold twice.

The marketplace

One place for the things that keep people well

Crystals and minerals, natural skincare, herbal and wholefood ranges, yoga and meditation supplies, books and gifts. Every listing belongs to an independent seller who packs and posts it themselves, and who is paid directly for what they sell.

Independent sellers, named

Each product carries the shop behind it. Buyers can follow a seller, read their reviews and see everything else they make.

Practitioners and events

Alongside the shop, a directory of practitioners and a listing for classes, workshops and retreats — bookable in the same place.

Paid directly, in full

Payments run through Stripe. Each seller is paid into their own account for their own orders, with fees itemised rather than netted off quietly.

For sellers

Connect the shop you already run

Most sellers who join us already trade somewhere else, and re-keying a catalogue by hand is the reason people never get round to a new channel. So we read your existing shop instead. You authorise Holistic Land from the platform you already use — no keys or passwords to copy across — and we bring the catalogue over for you to review before anything goes live.

Shopify

Products and delivery rates

Authorise us from your Shopify admin. Your products come across, and your delivery profiles are read too, so your postage prices here match the ones you already charge.

Etsy

Listings, and stock both ways

Your listings come across from your Etsy shop. When something sells on Holistic Land we write the new stock level back to Etsy, so you are not left selling the last one twice.

eBay

Listings and postage policies

Your eBay listings and your business postage policies are read directly from your account, and mapped onto the delivery options buyers choose from here.

Amazon

Your own Amazon account

Authorise Holistic Land from Seller Central and we work against your Amazon account, not ours. Listings map to the right Amazon product type for their category.

You review before it publishes

An import is a proposal, not a fait accompli. You see what we matched, change what we got wrong, and choose what to leave behind.

Nothing is locked in

Disconnect a shop whenever you like. Your Holistic Land listings stay yours, and we stop reading the other platform that day.

Your own delivery terms

Set your own postage rates and free-delivery threshold, or import the ones you already use. Buyers see them per seller at checkout.
